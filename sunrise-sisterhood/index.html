A morning that's just for you.

So many of us start the day already running. Lunches, messages, everyone else's plans.

Sunrise Sisterhood is a different way to begin. Women gather on the beach in the early light, pop on a pair of silent headphones and move, breathe and settle together, with the sound of the ocean underneath it all.

There's nothing to be good at. All ages, all bodies and all abilities are welcome. You just turn up, find a spot on the sand and let the morning do the rest.

How a sunrise session unfolds.

About an hour on the sand, then as long as you like over coffee.

01

Arrive

Come down to the beach in the soft pre-dawn light. Lay out your towel or mat, say hello and pick up your headphones.

02

Move

Gentle, flowing movement as the sun comes up. A little yoga, a little dance, all at your own pace.

03

Breathe

Breathwork and a guided meditation to finish, with the waves and the warmth on your face.

04

Coffee

Then the best bit for many of us. Coffee together, a lot of laughter and conversations with women you'll be glad you met.

Come alone, or bring a friend.

You don't need to know anyone. You'll leave knowing someone.
  • Come as you are.
  • Move as much or as little as you like.
  • Stay for coffee, or slip away to start your day.
  • Bring your mum, your sister or your best friend.
  • Come back next week and pick up where you left off.

More than a workout.

Sunrise Sisterhood was started on the Sunshine Coast by Tam Moroney, after losing her sister. She wanted a place where women could feel seen, supported and less alone.

It has grown into a community of women gathering on beaches up and down the east coast, from the Sunshine Coast to Moreton Bay and Sydney's Northern Beaches.

I'm so proud to bring it home to Rainbow Beach.

Why I lead Sunrise Sisterhood.

Like so many women, I've had seasons with a lot to carry. Raising my two boys, navigating separation, grief and loss, and all the things life brings along the way. I was so busy making sure everyone else was okay that I barely stopped to ask what I needed.

I know I'm not alone in that.

Rainbow Beach is my home. Every week I watch women arrive a little unsure, sometimes alone, with a gap between the towels. An hour later they're hugging, laughing and talking like old friends.

It's a beautiful reminder of how much we need these moments, and each other.

I'd love to see you on the sand. 🤎

You're probably wondering...

Can I come on my own?

Absolutely. Lots of women come on their own the first time. By the time the coffee arrives, you won't feel on your own at all.

Do I need to be fit, or have done yoga before?

Not at all. Sessions are gentle and welcome every age, body and ability. Go at your own pace and rest whenever you like.

What should I bring?

A towel or mat, a water bottle and something comfortable to move in. Headphones are provided.

What if the weather turns?

If a session is cancelled because of severe weather, you can transfer to another session or get a full refund.

What if I can't make it?

Let us know at least 24 hours before and you can transfer or get a refund. Cancellations inside 24 hours can't be refunded or transferred. Refunds take three to five business days.
